Privacy Policy for LazyStack Effective Date: July 30, 2026 Last Updated: July 30, 2026 LazyStack ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the LazyStack mobile application (the "App"). 1. Information Collection and Storage A. Local Document Processing & Data Storage Local Processing: All documents, PDFs, bookmarked files, reading progress, and local search indexes processed in LazyStack are handled locally on your device. No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) to external servers operated by us. 2. Permissions Required and How They Are Used LazyStack requests specific permissions strictly to enable core functionality: Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) Used exclusively for document scanning (OCR) to convert printed text into digital text within the App. Images captured by the camera are processed directly on your device and are never uploaded or shared for advertising, tracking, or profiling purposes. Storage / Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Media Store) Used solely to allow you to select and open PDF files or text documents saved on your device into LazyStack. Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET) Used to communicate with optional third-party web services (such as retrieving AI summaries via Gemini API or launching definition web searches) when explicitly triggered by you. 3. Third-Party Services & AI Processing LazyStack incorporates optional third-party integrations to enhance document reading and AI lookup features: Google Gemini API: When you request AI summaries, document structure analyses, or word definitions, relevant text snippets are securely transmitted to Google Gemini API servers. This processing is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Android System Text-to-Speech (TTS): Audio dictation uses your device's built-in Android TextToSpeech engine. Audio synthesis occurs locally or through your default system TTS provider per your Android system settings. External Web Searches: Quick word definitions opened via external browser tabs query standard search provider APIs (e.g., Google Search). We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or user tracking tools. 4. Children’s Privacy LazyStack is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove such data. 5. Security Because LazyStack stores your reading materials and preferences locally on your device, the security of your data relies on your Android device's built-in system security and encryption. 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this policy periodically for changes. 7.
