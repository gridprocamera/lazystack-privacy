Privacy Policy for LazyStack Last updated: 26 October 2026 LazyStack is a free book discovery and reading application designed to bring books and reading tools together in one place. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use it. 1. Our Privacy Approach LazyStack does not operate its own backend server or require users to create accounts. The app is designed around local storage and on-device reading. We do not operate a central server that collects your reading history, manages your personal library, or stores your offline books. Some features require an internet connection to access third-party book catalogs, retrieve book information, or download available books. These connections are made directly from the app to the relevant third-party services. 2. Information Stored on Your Device Depending on how you use LazyStack, the application may store: - Books and documents saved for offline reading. - Reading progress, bookmarks, and reading preferences. - App settings and cached book information. - Temporary files required to display and process documents. This information is stored locally in the application's storage or cache. App-private files are generally inaccessible to other ordinary applications on your device. Your personal reading library is not automatically uploaded to a LazyStack-operated server. Clearing app data or uninstalling the application may delete locally stored books, reading progress, preferences, and other app data. 3. Book Discovery and Third-Party Services LazyStack allows you to discover books from external catalogs and digital libraries. Depending on the feature, it may communicate with: - Project Gutenberg / Gutendex: To discover books and retrieve catalog information and available ebook links. - Internet Archive: To search its collection and retrieve book information and available files. - Open Library: To discover books and retrieve bibliographic information, metadata, and cover information. When you search for books or open a book listing, these services may receive information included in network requests, such as search terms, requested book identifiers, your IP address, and standard technical request information. These requests go directly from your device to the relevant third-party services. LazyStack does not operate an intermediary backend server for these requests. Each service has its own privacy policy, terms, and access restrictions. LazyStack does not control how those services handle information they receive. 4. Book Downloads and Offline Reading LazyStack supports offline reading of compatible books and documents. When a supported book is downloaded, the file is stored locally on your device in the application's storage. LazyStack does not operate a central ebook repository or upload your offline library to its own server. Book availability and download permissions depend on the original source. Some books may be unavailable, restricted, or accessible only through borrowing or other authorized reading methods. Users should respect the copyright status of individual works, applicable laws, and the terms of the source from which a book is accessed. 5. Documents You Import LazyStack supports reading compatible documents, including PDF and EPUB files, along with other supported text or scanned content. Document processing and reading operations supported by the app are designed to run on your device using local application code, libraries, and models. LazyStack does not intentionally upload your personal documents to a LazyStack-operated server. 6. Artificial Intelligence and Cloud Processing LazyStack does not use Gemini, external AI APIs, or cloud-based AI services to process books or documents. The app does not send document content to an AI service for text extraction, document analysis, or reading assistance. Supported on-device processing uses local application code, libraries, and models. Online book discovery and downloads may still communicate with the third-party book services described in this policy. 7. Analytics and Advertising LazyStack does not operate its own analytics or advertising server. The application does not intentionally send your reading history or personal offline library to a LazyStack-operated analytics service. Third-party services, Android system components, or libraries included in the application may process limited technical information according to their own practices. Such processing depends on the components and features included in the installed version. 8. Sharing of Information LazyStack does not sell your personal information. The app does not operate a service that shares your personal offline library or reading history with other users. It does not maintain a central account database containing your saved books or reading history. Information may be sent directly to external book services when you use online discovery, metadata, cover, or download features. Those services handle the information they receive according to their own policies. 9. Data Retention and Deletion Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or the information is otherwise removed by the app or operating system. You can remove saved books using the available application controls. Clearing app storage or uninstalling LazyStack may also remove other locally stored information. LazyStack does not maintain a central user account or personal reading-history database on its own servers. Third-party services may retain request information according to their own retention policies. 10. Children's Privacy LazyStack does not require an account or operate its own user-profile server. The application is not designed to collect personal information from children through a LazyStack-operated account system. However, third-party book services may have their own data practices, content policies, and age requirements. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external catalogs and book content where appropriate. 11. Security LazyStack relies on Android's application storage and security mechanisms to protect locally stored information. No method of electronic storage or internet communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices updated and use appropriate device security settings. 12. Third-Party Policies For further information about external services, consult their official policies and terms: - Project Gutenberg: https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/terms_of_use.html - Internet Archive: https://archive.org/about/terms.php - Open Library: https://openlibrary.org/privacy LazyStack is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by these services unless explicitly stated otherwise. 13. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the privacy-policy URL provided for LazyStack. 14. Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy or LazyStack's privacy practices, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the LazyStack Google Play listing.
